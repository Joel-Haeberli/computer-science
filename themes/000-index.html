Index

Welcome to the index. This is homebase. The Notes are based on the IMF Framework (get familiar with the concept first). Also have a look at the Best practices for documentation.

Index Categories

000s - Index, Terms, Organisations, Ports, Powers of 2, Documentation Framework: general & MOCs
100s - Applied Cryptography 1: symmetric cryptography
200s - Applied Cryptography 2: asymmetric cryptography
300s - Modern Cryptography: book summary “Introduction to Modern Cryptography” 1
400s - Distributed Systems Security: step by step explanation of Bitcoin
500s - Web Security: web security topics
600s - Security Protocols and Applications: network security and more
700s - Digital Forensics: evidence, acquisition, storage, tools
800s - Malware analysis and intelligence: identification, execution, hooking, persistence
900s - Exploiting and Defense: write exploits and explore defences

1000s - Joint Master in Computer Science: Topics covered in the Master degree in Computer Science.

1100s - Mathematics: A compilation of mathematical concepts used to understand what is going on.

1200s - Mathematical Modeling and Linear Optimization: Course about modeling real world problems in mathematical models

1300s - Discrete Mathematics: a bachelor course in discrete mathematics

1400s - Machine Learning: a bachelor course about Machine Learning

1500s - Computergraphic: Understand the representation of colors and light in computers

1600s - Applied Optimization: How can we optimize correctly?
